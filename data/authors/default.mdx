I’m Lindsay — a learning developer focused on the intersection where creativity, innovation, AI, and human-centered learning meet.

I’ve spent the last 11 years in L&D rescuing people from bloated content, sleepy courses, and the quiet despair of “click next to continue.”

This blog is where I explore the things I keep coming back to in L&D: useful design, creative problem-solving, AI, and the strange little art of making people care.

Outside of work, I’m usually reading, writing, spending time with my dogs, or painting Warhammer 40K miniatures — because apparently I enjoy big stories, tiny details, and a little creative chaos.
